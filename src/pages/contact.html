Before you ask

Most questions are answered in the FAQ and the developer hub.

  • Trouble signing in to a specific website? Contact that website. Nobody in the DigiByte community can access or recover an individual site's accounts.
  • Never share your recovery phrase. No legitimate helper will ask for it, in public or in private messages.
  • Security vulnerability? Report it privately via GitHub security advisories, not in a public issue.
